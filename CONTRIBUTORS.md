## Third-party work and future contributions

Andre Weissflog's floooh/chips and AJR's MAME Emulator II driver are credited
in README.md; their licenses and notices remain intact. Original E-mu hardware,
firmware and documentation are not authored by this project.

Future human and AI contributions should retain accurate attribution and
identify their scope. The 0.40 early golden milestone records working firmware,
disk loading, MIDI reception and audible playback; subsequent engineering
continues on performance, completeness and hardware fidelity.
